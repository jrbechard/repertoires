## Repertoires

Copy: no em dashes. Never "colour", always "color".
Terminology: "Community" for the public collection, "My repertoires" for
the user's own songs. Never "Library". "dim", not "o", for diminished.
French: Quebec spacing, narrow no-break space before `:`. Prices are `4 $`.

Color encodes pitch class only. Twelve hues, enharmonics share, quality
never shifts hue. The palette is identical in light and dark mode.
Palette overrides go on `:root`, never on `<body>`.
